GOLIDE Content Engine

Private publishing and analytics infrastructure for the G$LIDE YouTube channel.

What the application does

The GOLIDE Content Engine helps the channel owner prepare approved releases, upload videos privately, schedule publication and collect channel and video analytics for performance feedback.

Google OAuth is used only to authorize the channel owner's Google account. This is not a public sign-in service and does not provide accounts or OAuth access to other users.

Publishing

Approved media can be uploaded privately and scheduled only after production checks pass.

Analytics

Channel and video performance data is read to evaluate published content and improve future planning.

Data control

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected Google Account. Google user data is not sold.
